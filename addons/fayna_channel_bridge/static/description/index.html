Fayna Channel Bridge

Own direct transport for Odoo DM channels — no external intermediary, via the providers' native webhook/API.

What this module does

Fayna Channel Bridge is an autonomous transport layer that brings conversations from messengers straight into Odoo. The operator sees and replies to messages from all channels in one place — in Discuss and in the "Conversations" menu, without switching between apps.

  • channel.backend model — one record per channel + provider;
  • channel.conversation model — autonomous conversations;
  • channel.message model — journal with idempotency by provider_message_id (no duplicates);
  • Telegram webhook controller with signature protection;
  • Automation: healthcheck, retry with backoff, archiving old conversations.

Getting started

Open the "Connect channels" menu.
Choose a channel from the provider list.
Enter the access key (bot token or complete OAuth).
Done — conversations appear in Discuss and in the "Conversations" menu.
Who it is for: support operators and managers who want to reply to customers from all messengers in a single Odoo interface.
